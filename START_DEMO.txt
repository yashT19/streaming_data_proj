 WHY CLASSICAL WON (Quick Answer for Q&A)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. CPU SIMULATOR OVERHEAD
   Quantum circuits are simulated on CPU
   Every evaluation needs matrix math and optimization
   Classical just does linear algebra

2. DIFFERENT TRAINING STRATEGIES
   Classical learns from ALL 341 training records
   Quantum trains once, then just predicts
   Not an equivalent comparison

3. FEATURE REDUCTION
   Quantum limited to 4 qubits = 4 features
   Used PCA to reduce 30 → 4 (99.99% variance)
   Information loss on quantum side

4. HONEST ASSESSMENT
   CPU simulation ≠ Real quantum hardware
   Results specific to this setup
   Can't extrapolate to real quantum

→ KEY TAKEAWAY: Not quantum failure. Simulation overhead reality.
